# Reporting Issues

1. If something goes wrong, restart the computer first. If that does not help, download and extract the latest version: [GitHub](https://github.com/MAA1999/M9A/releases), [MirrorChyan](https://mirrorchyan.com/en/projects?rid=M9A&source=m9agh-anno3-en), or the [QQ group](https://qm.qq.com/q/CFEDGsEU1y).

2. Once you have ruled out a local issue, report it on [GitHub](https://github.com/MAA1999/M9A/issues) or in the [QQ group](https://qm.qq.com/q/CFEDGsEU1y).

3. Prepare your **reporting materials**. Developers cannot diagnose problems by guesswork; provide as much detail as possible so they can locate the issue quickly.

4. The most important materials are the `logs` and `debug` folders in the M9A directory. If needed, also record the software while the issue occurs.

5. If you report the issue in the QQ group, mention the trigger phrase `有问题看群公告，不行@我` and the group owner.
